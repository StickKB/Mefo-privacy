Mefo privacy policy

Updated 25 September 2026

What Mefo stores

Mefo is a free photo booth with no required account. Photos, optional silent source videos, finished keepsakes, imported reference guides, captions, private Journal notes and session settings are stored in the app on your device. Mefo has no media-upload service or advertising. Capture, editing and your Journal work without an internet connection.

Usage analytics and crash reports

Mefo uses PostHog for basic product actions, such as starting a session or completing a keepsake, with a random identifier for this installation. It uses Sentry for crash and error reports with technical details such as the app version, device model and iOS version. These services help us understand which features work and fix problems. They do not receive your photos, videos, imported guides, captions, notes, search text, face information or media file paths. Session replay, screenshots, advertising identifiers and cross-app advertising tracking are disabled.

Your analytics choices

Usage analytics and crash reports are on by default. In Journal → Privacy and help, turn off Share usage analytics or Share crash reports independently at any time. Your choices are saved on this phone. Turning a service off stops new collection. Requests already in progress may finish; reports already received are not deleted. PostHog and Sentry process reports on our behalf. Contact us with privacy or deletion questions. These settings do not change Apple's separate TestFlight diagnostics.

Camera and Photos

Camera access is requested when you enter capture or open an effects preview. Source video is optional and silent; Mefo does not request microphone access. Recreate uses Apple's photo picker to import only the image you choose, without requesting access to your whole library. Saving through the share sheet may request permission to add an image or video to Photos. You can change permissions in iPhone Settings.

Photo effects and face processing

Filters and optional Retouch run on your device. Mefo uses temporary face landmarks to apply effects; it does not identify you, create a face identity profile or send face information to a server. Landmark data is not saved with your sessions. Your original captures remain unchanged; generated keepsakes contain the effects you choose.

Sharing and backups

You decide when to share photos and videos using the system share sheet. The app or service you choose handles those copies under its own privacy practices. Journal notes and private reference guides are not included in exported keepsakes. Your app data may be included in ordinary device or iCloud backups according to your system settings; Mefo does not operate a separate cloud backup.

Retention and deletion

Sessions remain on your device until you delete them or remove the app. Use Journal's Select and Delete selected controls to delete sessions, including their media and notes. Session Storage controls can remove source videos or unused exports separately. Saved reference guides are stored separately and can remain after replacement or session deletion. Open Journal → Privacy and help → Reference storage to review reference usage and delete unused reference copies. References still used by your saved setup or existing sessions are protected. Removing a reference from setup stops using it in that setup; it does not erase a copy still needed elsewhere. Removing Mefo with Delete App removes all its local data, including reference guides; Offload App can retain it. Deletion does not remove copies you shared, saved to Photos or retained in backups.

Support and privacy contact

For help, privacy questions or feedback-deletion requests, email nickd3464@gmail.com. You can also open Journal → Privacy and help to open support email or copy the address. TestFlight is not required for support. Email you choose to send may include your address and any details or attachments you provide. We use this information to respond and investigate your request. Mefo does not automatically attach your photos, notes or reference guides. Avoid sending private content you do not want to share.

TestFlight beta testing

If you install Mefo through TestFlight, Apple operates that service and automatically shares beta crash logs and usage information with the developer under Apple's TestFlight privacy terms. Feedback you deliberately send may include contact details, screenshots and diagnostic information. We use beta diagnostics and feedback to investigate issues and support the beta. TestFlight testers can also open TestFlight, choose Mefo and tap Send Beta Feedback.

Policy updates

We will update this policy when Mefo's data practices change. Check the updated date above when installing a new version.
