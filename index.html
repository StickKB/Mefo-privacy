Mefo privacy policy

Updated 23 September 2026

What Mefo stores

Mefo is a free photo booth with no required account. Photos, optional silent source videos, finished keepsakes, imported reference guides, captions, private Journal notes and session settings are stored in the app on your device. Mefo has no media-upload service, advertising or app-operated analytics.

Camera and Photos

Camera access is requested when you enter capture or open an effects preview. Source video is optional and silent; Mefo does not request microphone access. Recreate uses Apple's photo picker to import only the image you choose, without requesting access to your whole library. Saving through the share sheet may request permission to add an image or video to Photos. You can change permissions in iPhone Settings.

Photo effects and face processing

Filters and optional Retouch run on your device. Mefo uses temporary face landmarks to apply effects; it does not identify you, create a face identity profile or send face information to a server. Landmark data is not saved with your sessions. Your original captures remain unchanged; generated keepsakes contain the effects you choose.

Sharing and backups

You decide when to share photos and videos using the system share sheet. The app or service you choose handles those copies under its own privacy practices. Journal notes and private reference guides are not included in exported keepsakes. Your app data may be included in ordinary device or iCloud backups according to your system settings; Mefo does not operate a separate cloud backup.

Retention and deletion

Sessions remain on your device until you delete them or remove the app. Use Journal's Select and Delete selected controls to delete sessions, including their media and notes. Session Storage controls can remove source videos or unused exports separately. Saved reference guides are stored separately and can remain after replacement or session deletion. Removing Mefo with Delete App removes all its local data, including reference guides; Offload App can retain it. Deletion does not remove copies you shared, saved to Photos or retained in backups.

TestFlight and contacting us

Apple operates TestFlight and automatically shares beta crash logs and usage information with the developer under Apple's TestFlight privacy terms. Feedback you deliberately send may include contact details, screenshots and diagnostic information. We use beta diagnostics and feedback to investigate issues and support the beta. To contact the developer about support, privacy or feedback deletion, open TestFlight, choose Mefo and tap Send Beta Feedback. Avoid attaching private content you do not want to share.
